(static) frustum(left, right, bottom, top, near, far) → {Float32Array}

General perspective frustum The perspective matrix is built as a product of three factors: 
  M_per = M_orth * P * F

  M_orth: Normalization from box to cube [l,r][b,t][n,f] -> [-1,1]^3
  P: Perspective warping, from frustum to box
  F: z-axis flip.
